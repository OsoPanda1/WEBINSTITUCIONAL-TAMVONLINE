01 — Identity

Who we are and with what limits

Name, mission, vision and a declaration of independence that does not depend on third parties.

Mission

Connect, document, preserve

To enable researchers, developers, creators and communities to organize their contributions and collaborate with traceability, ethical criteria and respect for authorship.

Vision

Latin American knowledge that endures

To build interoperable infrastructure where contributions can be identified, consulted, evolve and remain linked to their authors.

Declaration of independence

TAMV Online Network is not a unit, department, chair or subsidiary of another organization; it does not depend on an academic, governmental or commercial parent, and it does not receive such backing. The name describes the initiative and does not, by itself, constitute a claim of legal personality, academic accreditation or affiliation with third parties.

Membership · open call

Your work already exists. What is missing is a place where it can be verified.

We look for researchers, developers and makers whose affiliation field is empty and whose work already has form: a DOI, a licensed repository, a deposited dataset, a reviewable text. We do not ask for a degree or an institution; we ask that what you claim can be checked.

Read the call and its criteria Membership terms
  • A public TRI identifier with a registration date, linked to your ORCID and revocable.
  • A deposit with a DOI on Zenodo or OSF before formal publication, to fix authorship and date.
  • Review under the TQAF framework with a signed, numbered and appealable report.
  • A public registry entry: your name, your line of work and your output, citable by link.
  • Leaving costs nothing: on withdrawal you keep authorship and can require the seal to be removed.

TAMV is not a university and grants no degrees. It promises no indexing, no impact factor and no institutional endorsement: a third party decides those, not us.

How to read this site

Each claim indicates its source and its date of consultation. Where a datum is not available, the site writes not confirmed instead of filling it in with an assumption. This criterion is deliberate: a registry of research organizations is built on verifiable evidence, not on adjectives.
