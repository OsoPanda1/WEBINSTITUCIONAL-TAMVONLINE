HCU Living Universe

The living, traceable and governable state of the TAMV Online Network cognitive ecosystem, on one screen. Twenty-five particles —one conceptual anchor, six density regions and eighteen entities of the ecosystem—, seven relation propositions and a chronology of deposits. Each particle links the record that supports it or admits on screen that it does not have one yet.

01 — Concept

A field, not a list

A catalogue answers “which documents exist”. This map answers a different question: what exists, with what degree of evidence, and which dependencies mean a claim cannot yet be taken as valid.

The knowledge of a network is not a stack of files: it is a field where some pieces hold others up. That is why the smallest unit here is not a document but a traceable particle: something with a retrievable record, or something that expressly states it has none. An empty region is drawn with the same visual weight as a full one, because absence is also information a curator needs to see.

The rule that orders the map is a separation, not a scale:

CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY ≠ EXECUTION ≠ EVIDENCE ≠ LEARNING ≠ PRODUCTION.

Writing a mechanism down does not implement it. Implementing it does not authorise it. Authorising it does not prove it. Proving it once does not make it learning. And learning is not production: producing is leaving a record another person can consult tomorrow without asking permission.

What it is

  • Visible: it shows which entities of the ecosystem actually exist.
  • Explorable: any particle opens its dossier with the sources behind it.
  • Auditable: every source carries the date of the verification made on it.
  • Risk-observable: it flags where an exaggerated reading is likely.
  • Governance made tangible: it separates what may be authorised from what is only proposed.

What it is not

  • It is not a conscious artificial intelligence nor an agent that decides.
  • It grants no autonomous authority to any system or to this page.
  • It is not a physical or predictive simulation: it computes no futures.
  • It does not replace document management, the review file or the decision register.
  • It does not guarantee that what is published is true: it shows how far its evidence reaches.
The four movements of the map
  1. Simulation — the field is rendered as densities and flows so it can be read at a glance. It is a display aid, not a model of the world.
  2. Exploration — any region can be opened down to its entities, and any of those down to its full dossier.
  3. Selection — the dossier concentrates kind, activation, evidence state, seals, reading risk and the action that would raise the entity to the next state.
  4. Evidence — every source stays linked with its date; a downloadable snapshot preserves the exact state being viewed.

How each particle is drawn

  • ●Particle. Its size depends on the evidence density of that node alone: verified seals plus linked sources (capped at three). It does not depend on the importance of the topic.
  • ◌Dashed ring. Evidence state: widely spaced dots for E1, short dashes for E2, a continuous line for E3 or above. No entity of TAMV is at E3 today.
  • ◎Rose halo. High reading risk: the site considers it likely that a visitor over-reads what that particle claims.
  • ✓Outer marks. One per seal met: persistent identifier, declared licence, exact affiliation string, external review, public register.
  • ∅Declared void. A particle drawn on purpose where there is no output. It is a commitment not to backfill with borrowed evidence.
  • IA1Region code. Painted inside the particle: colour identifies the region but is never the only indicator.

The reading modes

A mode changes no data: it changes which layers switch on and therefore which question can be answered. Community: what exists and what is active. Research: where the voids and the traceability gaps are. Operations: what depends on what, and what is closed. Oversight: what may be authorised and what may not.

A fifth audience —the ecosystem itself, meaning other networks and curators— uses no mode: it reads the text register and downloads the snapshot.

What the canvas must not invite you to read

A particle position in space expresses no geographic proximity, economic value or likelihood of success. The movements are presentation animation, not the dynamics of a model. The starting layout is stable between visits: it derives from the order of the data, not from chance.

Relation propositions 7

Each flow is a hypothesis about how these entities condition one another. None claims causality: the line below states what would have to be observed to raise its state.

f1

condición documental

origin: GO1 · Institutional record: name, seat and identifiers · EQ3 · Licence regime of the corpus · CL2 · Deposited territorial atlas (Figshare 32508306)
destination: GO2 · ROR application dossier

Coherence of the affiliation chain is the condition for a curator to recognise the entity: records today showing variants ("TAMV ONLINE", "ENTERPRISE") weaken the dossier instead of strengthening it.

What would verify it. What would have to be observed to raise the state: corrections confirmed by each provider, with date, and a DataCite/OpenAlex search where the exact string appears without a competing variant.

f2

brecha estructural

origin: EQ2 · Open call for independent researchers · EQ1 · Network membership: zero approved profiles
destination: GO2 · ROR application dossier

A federated network is proven by outside people affiliating their output, not by architecture. Today public evidence shows a single identity signing the whole record.

What would verify it. Ten external authoring people with published affiliation in citable output, plus a member register with approved profiles and explicit consent.

f3

especificación versus ejecución

origin: IA2 · VETRA Ω∞ Canon v1.0 · IA4 · Fourteen VETRA support agents · IA1 · Isabella Villaseñor AI (GENESIS Ω∞ / UFCOS)
destination: IA3 · HCU Living Universe (this map)

Documenting a mechanism does not implement it. The agents and VETRA gates exist as text; the map can only reflect that state, and making it visible is its main function.

What would verify it. Independent test of the deployed scope, a report signed by an identifiable reviewer and a conflict declaration; without that, state 1 is the correct one.

Territorial memory only becomes research when it links primary sources and verifiable open data; without that it stays an identity statement.

Sign-up and proposal intake are closed because the legal basis is missing, not because code is missing. That is the difference between an unfinished product and a responsible one.

What each axis means

No value on this map is a measurement of importance. Density is computed from the seals and sources on the node itself; the other axes are attributed text or an outstanding action.

Evidence density
Computed: number of verified seals and linked sources on the node itself. It is not a measurement of the topic importance.
Systemic influence
Qualitative and attributed: high/medium/low band with the source behind it. The map does not turn it into a number.
Potential risk
Risk of misreading the node (exaggeration, dependence, absence), not global topic risk. It comes from each entity published text.
Uncertainty
Derived: it grows as evidence density falls. It is a visible consequence of the record, not an opinion.
Transformation potential
The action required to raise the state, written on each node. It is a task, not a score.

The five evidence states

The HCU-DEMO-1.0 scale. One and the same record can sit in a different state depending on the claim made about it.

  • E1 Hypothesis or proposed relation
  • E2 Source located
  • E3 Evidence assessed
  • E4 Replicated or independently reviewed
  • E5 Contested, superseded or withdrawn

04 — Method

What it demonstrates and what it does not

The map exists to make the evidence gap visible, not to close it with a good visualisation. These are the rules that keep it honest.

Construction rules

  • No particle is invented to complete the field: it exists because there is a record, or it is marked as a declared void.
  • No influence, risk or confidence number is written by hand as a measurement. What is shown as density is computed from the seals and sources on that node.
  • Every source carries the date of the verification actually performed on it; an old date is a warning, not decoration.
  • The evidence state uses the published HCU-DEMO-1.0 scale and is declared per entity, not per region.
  • The absence of a datum is not turned into a claim of non-existence: it is recorded as pending.

Snapshot integrity

The snapshot button exports the visible state and computes a local SHA-512 with Web Crypto over the JSON. It lets two readings of the map be compared: it matches or it does not.

It is not a digital signature, it does not bind the identity of whoever exported it, it provides no trusted timestamp and it does not prove that the claims in the graph are true.

Claims this map refuses

The published specification forbids declaring active BookPI roots, SHA3-512 seals, ISCIR Ω∞ certification, quantum entanglement or Kubernetes status, and this map does not emit them. It also runs nothing: the “ψ” label of other prototypes appears nowhere here because there is nothing to label.

Relation to existing transformation maps

The map takes from the interaction pattern popularised by the World Economic Forum Transformation Maps the idea of linking topics and showing their combined impact. The published HCU specification states explicitly that it does not claim to outperform the WEF and that no independent test of function, quality, scale or usability has been carried out. The difference here is one of purpose: the Forum maps order global topics; this one orders the state of a concrete corpus, with its sources and its voids in plain sight.

Evidence policy of this network: a website, an identifier, a DOI, a particle on a map, a citation count or a patent-search result do not by themselves prove peer review, technical functioning, organisational eligibility, patentability, authorship title, commercial value or legal compliance.
