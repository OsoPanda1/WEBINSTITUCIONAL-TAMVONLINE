03 — Affiliation

The exact string to use

A ROR curator, an index such as OpenAlex and an acknowledgments system compare text strings. Consistency is the evidence.

Correct form

In the affiliation field of any output: TAMV Online Network

With initial capitals, two words after TAMV, without "Enterprise", not all caps and without invented translations. When the form accepts city and country: Mineral del Monte, Hidalgo, México.

Forms that must stop being issued

  • TAMV ONLINE — appears in Zenodo deposits.
  • tamv online network — lowercase, detected in metadata.
  • TAMV Online Enterprise — the name of a third-party account, not of this network.
  • Nodo Cero and strings composed with "·" in CITATION.cff.

05 — Limits

What a DOI does not demonstrate

This distinction is what separates a serious dossier from a brochure. It applies to every claim on the site.

The record exists and is retrievableEntry with a resolvable DOI at the providerDemonstrated
The work is under an open licenseLicense field in the metadataDemonstrated in the cited deposits
The entity appears as an affiliationAffiliation string in the metadataDemonstrated with inconsistent variants
The work was peer reviewedIdentifiable third-party review recordNot demonstrated
The content is technically correctIndependent replication or published evaluationNot demonstrated

The evidence policy of this network is explicit: a website, an identifier, a DOI, a repository entry, a citation count or a patent search result do not by themselves prove peer review, technical operation, organizational eligibility, patentability, authorship ownership, commercial value or legal compliance. Each claim retains its source and its scope.

06 — Corrections

Traceability of changes

Incorrect metadata is corrected by declaring the change, not by silently replacing the previous version.

  1. The record and the erroneous field are identified, with a capture of its current state in the provider's API.
  2. The correction is opened through the provider's procedure (Zenodo, DataCite, Crossref, OpenAlex, Figshare) and the date is recorded.
  3. If the correction affects authorship or ownership, the consent of the people involved is required before touching the metadata.
  4. The change is recorded with version, reason and responsible party. A withdrawal is announced as a withdrawal; it is not disguised as an update.
  5. This page is updated with the new verification date once the provider confirms the change.
