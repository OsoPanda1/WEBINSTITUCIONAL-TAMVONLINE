Research lines and method

What TAMV Online Network investigates, the method by which evidence is classified and which projects exist today. Each line declares its scope and its actual maturity level; each project links the public source that supports it or admits that it does not have one yet.

02 — Method

Five evidence states

TAMV adopts the evidence model described in docs/hcu-methodology.md (HCU-DEMO-1.0, 2026-10-27). The same record can be in a different state depending on the claim made about it.

The method's starting rule

The document states it this way: a public record, a DOI, a repository or a citation establish that a record can be inspected; by themselves they do not validate the claims that record contains. Everything that follows serves not to confuse those two things.

  1. Hypothesis or proposed relation — a question to investigate. Supporting evidence has not yet been assessed. It is a legitimate state: most of TAMV's current work is here and is labeled as such.
  2. Source located — a primary or authoritative source is linked and its bibliographic datum is checked. Being linked does not mean it supports the claim.
  3. Evidence assessed — the method, population, scope, quality and limitations of that source are reviewed.
  4. Replicated or independently reviewed — there is independent evidence or an identifiable review process that supports the specific claim. None of the TAMV output consulted on 2026-11-01 had reached this state.
  5. In dispute, superseded or withdrawn — later evidence, a correction or a limitation changes the interpretation. Withdrawal is documented with reason and date.

Structure of the HCU Ω∞ prototype

Nodes
25Ω Core + 6 domains + 18 atoms. The domains are AI; economy and work; governance and rights; climate and territory; health and well-being; security and resilience.
Hyperedges
7Curated propositions that relate three or more topics at once. The document describes them as relation hypotheses, no as empirically validated causal relations.
Axes per node
5 illustrativeSystemic influence, potential risk, evidence coverage, uncertainty and transformation potential. The scheme keeps those dimensions separate instead of reducing them to a single "confidence" score.
Seeded values
Demonstration parametersThey are not measurements: they do not come from sensors, live datasets, scientific bases, laboratory tests or a VETRA committee. The time control is an exploration tool, not a forecast.

Local cognitive pods

They are session-only entities created in the visitor's browser. They do not bring up a container, a Kubernetes Pod, a model runtime or a service. The pod and quarantine actions modify the session's visible state; they are not production controls or persistent member records.

What the prototype demonstrates and what it does not demonstrate
  • Demonstrates a navigation pattern that makes uncertainty, evidence state and next questions visible.
  • Does not demonstrate a comparison with the Transformation Maps of the World Economic Forum: the document explicitly declares that no claim is made that HCU surpasses the WEF and that no independent test of functions, quality, scale or usability has been carried out.
  • Does not execute quantum computation: the "ψ" label is a visual indicator of phase. Nor does it declare active BookPI roots, SHA3-512 seals or ISCIR Ω∞ certification.
  • The integrity check of a snapshot is a SHA-512 computed locally over the JSON: it matches or it does not match. It is not a digital signature, it does not bind an author's identity, it does not provide a trustworthy timestamp and it does not demonstrate that the graph's claims are true.

HCU-DEMO-1.0 has no persistent graph database and no connected evidence register; its atoms and hyperedges do not yet have linked primary sources, and the scenario weights are not calibrated, cross-checked or validated. The VETRA framework is an internal pilot and has not independently certified HCU.

04 — Reproducibility

What a member must deposit

A work is reviewable when another competent person can understand what was done, why, under what criteria and how to evaluate or repeat it. The requirement is proportional to the project's phase.

Minimum deposit to enter review

  • Problem and research question, or verifiable technological need, with a falsifiable claim.
  • Hypothesis or requirements defined, with observable success criteria.
  • Method, experimental design, architecture or procedure, and state of the art with a comparison of relevant alternatives.
  • Data, code, configuration, versions or replication instructions when legally and technically possible; if not possible, justification of restricted access.
  • Tests carried out and results with source, environment, sample and limitations; log of errors, known failures and threats to validity.
  • In model results: version, configuration, evaluation set, metrics, uncertainty and failures.
  • Provenance of data, code, models, images and materials, with license and third-party permissions.
  • Declaration of the substantive use of AI tools in data generation, analysis, writing or code.
  • Funding, relevant relationships and declared conflicts of interest.
  • Reproducible future plan when results do not yet exist.

Early works are also admitted

The canon says it in these words: conceptual works and prototypes without complete results are accepted if they are labeled as such, the proposed method is assessable and they are not announced as verified results. The requirement is calibrated by maturity and risk, not by prestige or budget.

What the deposit does not do by itself

Separating demonstrated results, preliminary results, aspirations and outstanding work is mandatory in the dossier. Visual proximity between two nodes does not determine direction or strength; a numeric weight requires operational definition, data lineage and uncertainty analysis; a relation does not imply causality unless the method and the evidence justify it.

Boundary of the technical claims

A hash detects changes when the exact content is compared. By itself it does not demonstrate authorship, legal date, truth, chain of custody or publication. That is why the canon prohibits declaring active SHA3-512, Ed25519, ML-DSA, BookPI Root, timestamps or a public verifier until they are deployed, tested and documented.

A dossier may keep hashes, versions and provenance metadata; that makes it inspectable, not infallible.

No benchmarks are published without reproducible conditions, and systems are not compared with non-equivalent tasks or samples. Pilot results are reported with sample size, period, disciplines and missing data.

In the founder's ORCID record, the employment at TAMV ONLINE NETWORK appears with dates 2020-01-01 → 2026-01-01, that is, as a past position. While that source stays this way, the official evidence contradicts the network's claim of continuity and weakens this page. It must be updated to a current position without an end date before any application is sent.

What this page can claim and what it cannot
  • It does claim: that four declared lines exist, that there are deposits with DOI, that methodological documentation is published and that there is a written internal assessment framework.
  • It does not claim: that this output has been peer reviewed, that VETRA's thresholds are validated, that HCU measures anything, or that TAMV has a constituted committee.
  • It also does not claim that VETRA certifies works: its outputs are presented as internal TAMV assessments, and the canon itself prohibits using "international certificate", "VETRA accreditation" or equivalent seals until an independent and recognized process exists.
