Network membership

Membership exists for one concrete reason: so that a person who researches on their own can sign a stable, citable and verifiable affiliation, instead of leaving the affiliation field empty. This page states what it provides today, what is in preparation and what it does not provide in any case.

01 — The problem we solve

Without affiliation, a contribution becomes invisible

This is not a metaphor. The affiliation field is the key with which identification systems group the output of a person and of an organization. When it is left empty, the work is not aggregated anywhere.

The metadata is missing

Empty or optional affiliation

A DOI in DataCite or Crossref can be issued without an institution in the metadata. The publication exists and is recoverable, but no aggregator links it to an organization: it remains authorship without institutional context.

The stable signature is missing

Pseudonyms and homonyms

A personal name or an email address does not resolve ambiguity. Without a stable name to write, two people with a common surname are confused and the same person appears as several.

The organizational route is missing

Identifiers that do not cover the gap

ORCID identifies people and ROR identifies organizations. An independent researcher may have an ORCID in good order, but has no organizational entity of their own to cite in the affiliation field.

How a documented network resolves this

Affiliation becomes citable when it is verifiable

An affiliation string works if it points to a public record with a fixed name, location, governance, output and a contact channel a third party can verify. One person alone does not invent that record: it is built from the sum of the outputs signed by its members. Membership is the mechanism for generating that evidence collectively and honestly.

03 — Admission requirements

What is declared when applying

The requirements come from the first gate of VETRA Ω∞ — integrity, authorship and provenance. They are verifiable declarations, not credentials: no degree, no prior affiliation and no institutional history are requested.

Required declarations

  • Honest authorship. Real contributions and co-authorships. Coauthors, affiliations, reviewers, funders and endorsements are not invented.
  • Provenance of the materials. Origin of data, code, models, images and text, with licenses and third-party permissions identified.
  • Declared conflicts of interest. Funding and relevant relationships. State expressly if none exist.
  • Rights over what is published. Hold the authority to disclose the material and declare the license when one exists.
  • Separation of claims. Distinguish demonstrated results, preliminary results, hypotheses and outstanding work. Novelty is stated as a hypothesis while it has not been demonstrated.
  • Declared AI use. Every substantive use of AI tools in authorship or in production must be declared. A model's output does not convert an unchecked reference into evidence.

Blocking causes

Plagiarism or deliberate fabrication of data, falsified authorship, invented references, material concealment of conflicts, disclosure of protected content without authorization or substantiated documentary fraud. High completeness does not compensate for falsification: the framework is designed to close in these cases.

What is not asked

No academic degree, no prior publication, no backing institution and no particular language are required. Admission evaluates the honesty and traceability of what the person declares, not their curriculum.

04 — What it does NOT include

The limits of membership

This section exists so that no one — a member, a curator or a reader — can draw a conclusion that does not correspond to reality.

Belonging to the network grants none of this
  • Academic accreditation. TAMV Online Network is not an accredited institution, and membership confers no official academic value on a degree or on a study.
  • Certification. There is no "international certification" from the network. VETRA Ω∞ is an internal pilot framework, not an accredited body, and its self-assessment is not a ruling.
  • Employment, salary, grants or awards. No amount, contract, grant, remuneration or financial backing is promised for being a member.
  • Acceptance of a ROR ID. Eligibility and the decision belong exclusively to ROR. No membership, payment or tier accelerates or guarantees a curation approval.
  • Indexing. It is not guaranteed that any aggregator, database or journal will index, cite or accept an output for carrying this affiliation.
  • Automatic entry to projects. Collaborating on a project requires express agreement; membership gives no right to appear in others' work or to use the network's name for personal benefit.
  • Legal personality. The network is not incorporated as a legal entity and does not publish an RFC; affiliation is not an employment or corporate bond.

What it does include, and this is the important part

A stable and published name, a verifiable location, written governance rules, a contact channel, and the possibility that one's own output becomes aggregated into a queryable record. Nothing more, but that much with sources.

05 — The process

How an application is processed

The designed flow has six stages. Automated intake is in preparation: until it is open and verified, applications are handled by email.

  1. Application. The person writes to the institutional channel with their name, country, lines of work and links to existing output.
  2. Dossier. Authorship, provenance of materials, conflicts, rights and licenses are declared, and the demonstrated is separated from the preliminary.
  3. Review. A responsible person examines the dossier against the first gate of VETRA. The review is human.
  4. Reasoned decision. A written reason is returned: admission, missing information or rejection. Decisions are recorded.
  5. Publication with consent. The profile remains private by default. It is published only with the person's express consent and a recorded approval.
  6. Appeal. A rejection or a suspension can be challenged in writing before a reviewer other than the one who decided.

Status of the automation

The registration form and VETRA proposal intake are disabled by default. Before they open, applied migrations, RLS verification, an approved privacy and retention policy, and a documented committee and appeal process are required. A commit in the repository does not configure a database and does not open intake.

That is why, today, the functional route is email. Any page on this site that describes automatic registration marks it as planned or in preparation.

Timelines

We do not publish a response time or a review duration: the operating process is not yet documented as a service. A time target will be announced when one exists. See contact.
