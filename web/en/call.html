Open call for independent researchers and developers

We look for people who already publish — with a DOI, with open-source code, with deposited data — and who still leave the affiliation field empty because they have no institution to write there. This page states precisely what the network offers, what it cannot yet offer, and what is reviewed before affiliating someone. It does not promise what it cannot sustain.

01 — The gap

Why this open call exists

The problem is not the quality of the work. It is that the academic registration system groups output by institution, and someone who belongs to none appears scattered: their articles exist, are citable, and still are not aggregated anywhere.

The empty field

Affiliation: not written

A deposit in Zenodo or Figshare can be published without institutional affiliation. The DOI resolves, the work exists, and no aggregator links it to an organization. It is work made invisible to the collective record.

The closed door

Identifiers that do not cover this

ORCID identifies people. ROR identifies organizations. An independent researcher may have a flawless ORCID and still have no legitimate organizational entity to cite. No one is going to hand them one along the way.

What is missing

A documented network, not a brand

An affiliation is only useful if it points to a verifiable public record: a fixed name, a location, written governance rules, real output, and a contact channel a third party can check. One person alone does not invent that. It is built from the sum of what its members sign.

Starting state, declared without adornment

As of 2026-11-01 the network has one single person with a TAMV affiliation verifiable in public sources: its founder. A public member directory does not exist, and automated registration remains disabled. Someone who responds to this open call does not join a body of researchers already registered: they enter to build the missing evidence, under rules written in advance. That is what is offered, and it is also the limitation.

02 — Profile

Who we look for

Criteria of traceability, not of curriculum. No degree, no qualification, no backing institution, no language and no country are requested.

You fit if you already do this

  • You published or deposited something with a persistent identifier: a DOI in Zenodo, Figshare, DataCite, OSF, or a software repository with an explicit license.
  • You maintain documented open-source code, curated data, a study protocol, or digital heritage material with clear provenance.
  • You can name where your data, code, models and images came from, with licenses and permissions.
  • You work on a line the network declares active: territorial memory and culture, software and open infrastructure, responsible AI, research and open science.
  • You are willing to declare the substantive use of AI tools in your process, if there was any.
  • You seek real co-authorship, not appearing in someone else's work.

You also fit if you are starting

A conceptual work or a prototype enters the network if it is labeled as such. The criterion is not "I already have results", it is "I can distinguish what I demonstrated from what I propose". The demand for evidence is calibrated by the phase of the project and by its risk, not by the prestige of whoever signs.

Profiles of explicit interest

  • Researchers without a position who publish on their own.
  • Developers of scientific software or of infrastructure with active repositories.
  • Data curators, archivists, and documenters of local heritage.
  • Reviewers with disciplinary competence willing to review others' work.
  • Student researchers with declared supervision.
What is not a requirement, and why we say it

A university degree is not required, nor prior publication in an indexed journal, nor affiliation to another institution, nor membership in a country or language. Requiring that would reproduce exactly the barrier this network tries to open. What is required is honesty about what was done and traceability of where it came from.

04 — Limits

What this open call does not offer

Read this section before writing. It is the part that prevents misunderstandings, and the part an experienced reader will look for.

Being accepted into the network grants none of this
  • Academic accreditation. TAMV Online Network is not an accredited institution and confers no official academic value on outside degrees or studies.
  • Certification. There is no "international certification". VETRA Ω∞ and TQAF are internal frameworks; their outputs are rulings of TAMV, not third-party endorsement.
  • Employment, grant, salary or award. None of that is promised for being a member.
  • Payment of APCs, travel or expenses. The network does not fund publications or travel of its members. If a member needs to cover an article charge, that is negotiated with the external editor, not with TAMV.
  • Acceptance of a ROR application. Eligibility and the decision belong exclusively to ROR's curation. No membership, payment or tier accelerates or guarantees that decision.
  • Indexing. It is not guaranteed that any database, aggregator or journal will index, cite or accept a work for carrying this affiliation.
  • Automatic entry to projects. Collaborating requires express agreement. Membership gives no right to appear in others' work or to use the network's name for personal benefit.
  • Legal personality. The network is not incorporated as a legal entity. Affiliation is not an employment or corporate bond.

And what it does offer, which is the important part

A stable and published name where there was an empty field, evaluation rules written before evaluating, human review with a reasoned decision, the possibility that your output is aggregated into a queryable record, and the company of other people in the same situation. Nothing more. But that much, yes, with sources.

05 — Applying

How to respond to the open call

Today the functional route is email. The registration form exists in the platform code and remains disabled by default until migrations are applied, access control is verified, the data controller is published and retention is approved. A commit does not open an intake.

  1. Write to the institutional channel with the subject line "Open call — [your area]". Include the name you sign with, country and city, and the lines you work on.
  2. Link your existing output. DOIs, repositories, profiles in ORCID, Zenodo, Figshare, OSF, GitHub or DataCite. Without links there is nothing to review.
  3. Declare provenance and rights. Where data, code, models, images and text came from; what licenses they carry; whether you need a third party's permission.
  4. Declare conflicts and AI use. Funding and relevant relationships — or that none exist —. Every substantive use of AI in data, analysis, writing or code.
  5. Separate the demonstrated from the proposed. Proven results, preliminary results, hypotheses and outstanding work, separately. Novelty is stated as a hypothesis while it has not been demonstrated.
  6. Indicate what you seek. Affiliation to sign your own work, co-authorship, review by a third party, or participation in an existing line.

What the network does with your message

A responsible person examines the dossier against the first gate of VETRA Ω∞ — integrity, authorship and provenance — and responds with a written decision: admission, missing information or reasoned rejection. Decisions are recorded. A rejection can be challenged before a reviewer other than the one who decided.

We do not publish a response time. The process is not documented as a service with timings, and announcing one would be an empty promise.

What is blocked without exception

  • Plagiarism or deliberate fabrication of data.
  • Falsified authorship or invention of coauthors, reviewers or endorsements.
  • Deliberately invented references.
  • Material concealment of conflicts of interest.
  • Disclosing protected content without the owner's authorization.

Privacy of the application

The profile is born private. Nothing is published without express consent. Do not send passwords, tokens, sensitive data or third-party material you cannot disclose. Mail verification is not verification of civil identity.

06 — Route

What has to happen, in order

The open call is not an image campaign: it is the mechanism for producing the collective evidence that is missing today. This is the sequence with its checkable milestones.

Why the order matters

Requesting ROR with a single person exposes the network to the record's explicit exclusion criterion: single-person organizations. Sending early not only fails; it leaves a precedent. The correct route is the one you have already begun: first the collective evidence, then the application. The field-by-field detail is in the application dossier.

What this page does not claim
  • It does not claim that the network has members, an incorporated committee, a public directory or an operating register.
  • It does not claim that a work affiliated with TAMV has been peer-reviewed, replicated or endorsed by third parties.
  • It does not claim that responding to the open call leads to an accreditation, a ROR ID or a publication.
  • It claims that there are written rules, verifiable output of the founder, a contact channel and a documented route. That is all it can sustain today.
