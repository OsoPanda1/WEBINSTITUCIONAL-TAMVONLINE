TAMV Quality Assurance Framework

TQAF is the internal system the network uses to decide what it publishes, at what level of review and with what evidence. It does not promise absolute excellence: it promises a public, demanding and auditable quality control.

01 — Guiding principle

Excellence is not declared

No serious organization can guarantee scientific quality in absolute terms, because science advances through review, criticism, replication, correction and accumulated evidence.

Excellence is not declared. It is documented, reviewed, corrected and preserved.
Institutional motto of TAMV Online Network

What the network can and must do is publish the verifiable criteria by which each result is evaluated: scientific integrity, methodological rigor, documentary traceability, reproducibility, transparency, responsible attribution, technical or peer review, FAIR metadata, persistent identification, digital preservation, public correction of errors and retraction when appropriate.

Relationship with the VETRA Ω∞ canon

VETRA is the evaluation canon: it defines the three mandatory gates (integrity and provenance; method and reproducibility; responsibility, rights and impact) and the principles that support them. TQAF is the operating system: it turns those gates into output levels, enforceable standards, an editorial flow and a seal. VETRA without TQAF would be philosophy; TQAF without VETRA would be mere formality.

02 — Levels

Three levels of assurance

The level is declared before reviewing, not after. An institutional document is not evaluated with the same requirements as a research article.

TQAF applies to preprints, articles, technical documents, whitepapers, reports, datasets, software, protocols, methodological guides, case studies, architectures, digital heritage materials and technical presentations.
LevelDesignationWhat it coversRequired review
N1Institutional documentationManuals, policies, announcements, operational guidesBasic editorial review
N2Verifiable technical documentationArchitectures, protocols, software, technical reportsDocumented technical review
N3Academic outputPreprints, articles, datasets, studies, whitepapersExpert or peer review + editorial control

Operating status

Today the network publishes the policy and the dossier, and declares plainly that it does not yet have archived review minutes or a committee with real members and declared conflicts. Until that exists, the correct designation of the function is editorial coordination in consolidation, and no output may be shown with the seal from section 06.

03 — Standards

TQAF-10

Ten mandatory standards. Each one declares its minimum requirement, which is what makes the difference between a principle and a control.

Standard 1

Scientific integrity

No fabricated data, no falsified results, no manipulation of evidence; correct citations, limitations and conflicts declared.

Minimum: declaration of integrity and of conflicts of interest.

Standard 2

Problem and justification

What problem it addresses, why it is relevant, what gap it attends to, what its scope is and what its limits are.

Minimum: introduction, context, justification and scope.

Standard 3

Traceable method

Approach, sources, tools, procedures, inclusion and exclusion criteria, analysis, limitations and known biases.

Minimum: reproducible or auditable methodological section.

Standard 4

Verifiable evidence

References, data, code, documentation, repositories, primary sources, experimental results or technical records.

Minimum: verifiable references and supporting evidence.

Standard 5

Reproducibility

Data, code, parameters, instructions, versions, technical requirements and replication procedure when legal and possible.

Minimum: full / partial / restricted / not applicable declaration.

Standard 6

FAIR metadata

Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable: persistent identifier, access by standard protocol, shareable vocabularies and clear license.

Minimum: DOI, complete metadata, license and provenance.

Standard 7

Authorship and contribution

Explicit roles: conceptualization, methodology, software, validation, formal analysis, investigation, curation, writing, editing, visualization, supervision and administration.

Minimum: CRediT table or equivalent declaration.

Standard 8

Documented review

Reviewer identifier, date, type of review, observations, editorial decision, revised version and approval date.

Minimum: internal review minutes or editorial control record.

Standard 9

Normalized affiliation

Exact name TAMV Online Network, location, each author's ORCID when it exists, each member's TRI where applicable and ROR when obtained.

Minimum: exact affiliation + ORCID + TRI where applicable.

Standard 10

Preservation and correction

Initial version, change history, DOI, license, correction mechanism, retraction policy and preserved archive.

Minimum: versioning + DOI + public correction policy.

04 — Flow

Editorial pipeline in thirteen steps

Each step leaves a record. None is skipped for seniority, financial contribution or membership level.

  1. Output proposal.
  2. TQAF classification: N1, N2 or N3.
  3. Verification of authorship and affiliation (TRI + ORCID + exact string).
  4. Integrity review · gate 1 of VETRA.
  5. Methodological or technical review · gate 2.
  6. Editorial review.
  7. Corrections.
  8. Final approval with human oversight.
  9. DOI assignment in the corresponding repository.
  10. Verifiable publication.
  11. Registration in the network's catalog.
  12. Linking with ORCID, TRI and ROR when it exists.
  13. Monitoring, correction and preservation.

Possible decisions

DecisionMeaning
AcceptedMeets the applicable TQAF criteria.
Accepted with minor changesRequires editorial or technical adjustments.
Accepted with major changesRequires additional review before publishing.
RejectedDoes not meet the minimum criteria of quality, ethics or traceability. The rejection is reasoned in writing.
WithdrawnThe author requests withdrawal or there is a documented ethical cause.
CorrectedA corrected version is published with a visible history.
RetractedSerious evidence of misconduct, irreparable error or ethical problem.

Blocks with no numerical appeal

  • Plagiarism or deliberate fabrication of data.
  • Falsified authorship or attribution to a person who did not contribute.
  • Deliberately invented references.
  • Material concealment of conflicts of interest.
  • Lack of authorization to disclose protected content.
  • Substantiated documentary fraud.

A critical failure is not compensated by a good aggregate score: the gates are gates, not averages.

05 — Authorship

Who may sign and who may not

Authorship is earned with substantial contribution, not with hierarchy, seniority or payment.

An author is one who contributed substantially to

  • Conceptualization or methodological design.
  • Data collection or curation.
  • Software development, analysis or interpretation.
  • Writing of the manuscript or critical revision of the content.
  • Approval of the final version and responsibility over one's own contribution.

One contributes without being an author by providing

  • Technical support, editorial review or one-off data curation.
  • Acknowledgments, technical collaborations or institutional resources.
  • Funding or infrastructure, which is declared as such.
Use of generative artificial intelligence
  • AI may support writing, translation, exploratory analysis, documentation or code.
  • AI cannot be an author and authorship cannot be attributed to it.
  • The signing persons are responsible for verifying all AI-generated or AI-assisted content.
  • Use with substantive impact is declared in the dossier.
  • No model output becomes evidence by the fact of being cited: an unchecked reference remains unchecked.
06 — Seal

TAMV Verified Output

A label with strictly delimited meaning. Its value depends on saying what it includes and what is left out.

TAMV Verified Output
Nivel: N3 — Output académico
Fecha de verificación: YYYY-MM-DD
Versión: 1.0
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX
TRI del responsable: TRI-TAMV-YYYY-XXXX
Afiliación: TAMV Online Network, Real del Monte, Hidalgo, México
Criterios aplicados: TQAF-10
Estado: Publicado y sujeto a corrección pública

Correct meaning

This output met the internal criteria of review, traceability, attribution and publication of TAMV Online Network on the indicated date.

Prohibited meaning

This output is scientifically definitive, infallible or validated by the international academic community. Using the seal with that meaning is expressly forbidden.

The seal is a self-declaration of internal process, not an accreditation. VETRA is not a certified body and this site does not present it as such: until the process is implemented, tested and governed independently, decisions are presented as internal rulings of TAMV. A third party seeking the external validation that the seal does not contain must be able to discover it by reading this page.

07 — Correction

Correcting and retracting in public

The difference between a serious network and a showcase lies in what it does when it makes a mistake.

Minor correction

Without altering conclusions

Errata, typographical errors, incorrect metadata, broken links, format adjustments.

Major correction

May affect the interpretation

Corrected data, adjusted method, updated results or changes in the conclusions.

Retraction

The work no longer stands

Fabrication or falsification, grave plagiarism, fraudulent authorship, serious ethical violation, undue duplication or irreparable error.

Every correction or retraction is public, dated and linked to the original DOI. A withdrawal is announced as a withdrawal: the previous version is not silently replaced and the record is not deleted.

What a verification dossier contains

Initial proposal · submitted version · review report · corrections made · final version · approval minutes · DOI metadata · license · contribution declaration · conflict declaration · reproducibility declaration · version history · catalog registration · persistent identifiers. The dossier does not need to be entirely public, but it must exist and be auditable.
